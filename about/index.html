Technologist

This is at the center of everything I do. Even my projects sorted under other disciplines are built on the foundation of technology. In addition to enabling my projects, it also lets me build bridges between them. My makerspace work allows me to build enclosures for the light fixtures that accentuate my musical performances, as one example. What would otherwise be separate disciplines become one connected body of work.

Designer

If technology is the foundation of my work, design is the lens. It shapes how I see the world and how I want my work to be seen. Good design can be anywhere, improve anything — it's a consideration when I build a website, assemble a DJ set, and draft an email.

Musician

This one's mainly for me. Since I stopped DJing full time in 2020, music hasn't been much of a consideration with regard to my career path. Even so, it's important enough to me on a personal level that it warrants a spot here, especially considering how often it's involved in my other projects.
