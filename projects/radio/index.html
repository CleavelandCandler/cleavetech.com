Radio — House Mountain Beat Factory

Fall 2022 - A few weeks into my job in W&L's ITS department, I was still making my way around campus and meeting all my new clients. One such client was the manager of our college radio station, WLUR. We got to talking, I filled him in on my background as a DJ, and he offered me a slot then and there.

For the next eight months, I crafted and aired eighteen episodes of my show, House Mountain Beat Factory. The real House Mountain was visible from my studio window at the time, and I often found myself gazing at it whenever I was struggling with a transition or just needed inspiration. Its iconic silhouette became the base for the show's logo.

Eventually, I became too occupied with other projects to continue producing HMBF at a consistent rate. I wrapped the 18 episodes as "Season One" and put the show on hiatus. I still collect and organize music for it, and fully expect to return for a Season Two one day.
